How to Enter Status Reports That Produce Reliable Metrics

The dashboard works best when each report represents one real delivery item with an owner, reporting week, stage, due date, risk, and next action. This guide explains how to turn loose updates into management-ready data for dashboards, meetings, and weekly feedback.

1. Structure

Use Product for the main product and Feature / workstream for the specific delivery being tracked.

2. Update

During the same week, edit the existing report. In a new week, create the weekly status for the workstream.

3. Decide

Every blocker needs a next action, accountable owner, and target date to become manageable.

Core Rule

1

One report = one product + one workstream + one owner + one reporting week

Do not mix several workstreams into one report. Do not create a new product for every feature. This rule prevents duplication and makes active products, progress, stage mix, delays, risks, and owner workload reliable.

Stage, Health, And Progress

Stage explains where the work is.

Health explains whether delivery is safe or at risk.

Progress explains how much is complete.

Example: a workstream can be in QA Review, at 70% progress, and still be At Risk if there is a technical blocker.

Schedule Classification

The end date should reflect the real expected delivery date.

Ahead of Schedule delivery moved earlier than baseline.

On Time delivery remains within expected timing.

Minor Delay delay up to 10%.

Major Delay delay above 10%.

Practical Reporting Scenarios

ScenarioWhat to captureDashboard reading
External blocker Depends on, blocker/risk, delay root cause, corrective action owner, action target date. Appears in Incidents & Delays, Delivery Timeline, Action Items, and Manager Meeting.
Scope change Baseline end date, new end date, date change reason, decision needed. Explains whether the movement is approved replanning or unmanaged delay.
High progress but at risk Progress, stage, health, blocker/risk, decision needed, next action. Prevents high progress from hiding an unresolved delivery risk.
Partial delivery Win, remaining blocker, milestone, next action, stage. Shows that work moved forward but is not delivered yet.
Delivered workstream Stage = Completed, Progress = 100, final summary, win, no open delivery blocker. The workstream is treated as delivered.
Dependency between workstreams Depends on, blocker/risk, next action, owner of the dependency. Highlights possible critical path items in Delivery Timeline.

Weekly Cadence

Monday

Create or update weekly reports. Confirm active workstreams, stage, dates, and dependencies.

Wednesday

Review blockers, update next actions, and escalate pending decisions.

Friday

Close the weekly status, mark Completed when delivered, and confirm action owner plus target date.

Golden Rule For Status Meetings

For each product, be able to answer in less than 60 seconds: overall status, dominant stage, progress, delay, blocker, impact, next action, and decision needed.

If the report does not answer those questions, improve it before using it in a meeting.
